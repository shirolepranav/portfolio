# Tier step-down (src/world/governor.ts)

Rule: drawn frames only (not hidden, not the first 2 s, not the dive). A full 3 s window averaging under 45 fps steps down one tier;
then 3 s ignored and a fresh 3 s window. A lone gap of 1 s or more between fast frames is a pause and restarts the window;
two long gaps in a row are slowness. Never steps up. On low, under 30 fps reports "still" once (the landing shows its still).
The stepped tier is kept in sessionStorage (lake:tier) for the browser session.

## 1. Synthetic frame streams (node scripts/governor-test.ts)

steady 60 fps                              ends high   no change
steady 46 fps (just above the line)        ends high   no change
steady 40 fps                              ends low    5.03 s high->medium (40.0 fps), 11.03 s medium->low (40.0 fps)
40 fps, then 60 fps after the first step   ends medium 5.03 s high->medium (40.0 fps)
swings 30/60 fps every second              ends low    5.50 s high->medium (44.9 fps), 11.51 s medium->low (44.9 fps)
swings 40/50 fps every 2.5 s               ends low    5.02 s high->medium (41.6 fps), 11.04 s medium->low (43.4 fps)
one 1.2 s frozen frame, otherwise 60       ends high   no change
one 2 s stall at 5 fps, otherwise 60       ends medium 11.00 s high->medium (41.5 fps)
steady 0.8 fps (every gap over 1 s)        ends low    8.75 s high->medium (0.8 fps), 17.50 s medium->low (0.8 fps), 26.25 s low->still (0.8 fps)
1.2 s frozen frames every 10 s, else 60    ends high   no change
steady 20 fps (landing)                    ends low    5.10 s high->medium (20.0 fps), 11.10 s medium->low (20.0 fps), 17.10 s low->still (20.0 fps)
